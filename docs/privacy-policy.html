Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Johntalks Media ("we," "our," or "us") is a digital media and content platform designed to empower creators, businesses, and audiences through content distribution, engagement tools, analytics, and monetization systems.

We are committed to protecting your privacy while delivering a personalized, secure, and value-driven experience.

By using Johntalks Media, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three main categories:

A. Information You Provide

  • Account details (name, email, username, password)
  • Profile information (bio, profile image, preferences)
  • Content you upload (videos, text, images, audio)
  • Messages and communications within the platform
  • Payment or payout details for monetization features

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (IP address, browser type, operating system)
  • Usage data (pages viewed, time spent, clicks, interactions)
  • Location data (general region, not precise GPS unless permitted)
  • Cookies and tracking technologies

C. Third-Party Information

  • Login data from connected platforms (Google, TikTok, Instagram, etc.)
  • Analytics from advertising or affiliate networks
  • Embedded content interactions

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to operate and grow Johntalks Media, including:

Core Platform Functions

  • Account creation and authentication
  • Content hosting and distribution
  • Community interaction features (likes, comments, shares)

Personalization & AI Optimization

  • Recommending content based on behavior
  • Tailoring feed experiences
  • Improving engagement through machine learning insights

Monetization & Business Growth

  • Enabling ads, sponsorships, and affiliate systems
  • Helping creators earn from content
  • Providing performance analytics dashboards
  • Matching brands with relevant creators

Platform Improvement

  • Debugging and performance monitoring
  • Feature development and testing
  • Security and fraud prevention

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal identity data.

However, we may share information in the following cases:

A. With Business Partners

  • Advertisers (non-identifiable or aggregated data)
  • Brand collaboration partners
  • Analytics providers

B. For Legal Compliance

  • If required by law or government request
  • To protect users, creators, or platform integrity

C. With Service Providers

  • Cloud hosting services
  • Payment processors
  • AI and recommendation engine providers

D. Aggregated Insights

We may share anonymized insights for:

  • Market trends
  • Content performance reports
  • Advertising optimization

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Analyze user behavior
  • Improve recommendations
  • Serve relevant ads

You may disable cookies, but some features may not function properly.

6. Advertising and Monetization

Johntalks Media supports a creator-first monetization model:

We may use your data (in anonymized form) to:

  • Deliver targeted advertising
  • Match brands with creators
  • Improve ad performance and revenue potential

Creators may earn through:

  • Ad revenue sharing
  • Sponsored content opportunities
  • Affiliate links
  • Digital product sales

7. Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard protections including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Secure cloud infrastructure
  • Access controls and authentication layers
  • Continuous monitoring for breaches

However, no system is 100% secure, and users share data at their own risk.

8. User Rights and Controls

You have control over your data:

  • Access your personal data
  • Edit or delete your profile
  • Download your data
  • Opt out of personalized ads (where available)
  • Request account deletion

9. Data Retention

We retain data:

  • As long as your account is active
  • As needed for legal compliance
  • As necessary for platform improvement and fraud prevention

Deleted accounts may retain anonymized analytics.

10. Children's Privacy

Johntalks Media is not intended for users under the age of 13 (or applicable local digital consent age). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. International Users

Your data may be stored and processed in multiple countries. By using the platform, you consent to cross-border data transfers.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes through the platform or email.
